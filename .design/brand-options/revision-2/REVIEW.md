# Logly identity: originality screening and replacement concepts

**Update — 2026-09-09:** User selected A — Offset Register and authorized implementation. The normalized vector is in `packages/ui/src/brand.tsx`; implementation details are in `.brain/features/brand-identity.md`. The screening limits below remain unchanged.

Reviewed 9 September 2026. Recommendation: **A — Offset Register**, provisionally, for visual development. None of the names or symbols is legally cleared or guaranteed globally unique.

## What was actually checked

Reviewed the five existing concept boards and three newly generated boards. Ran public web searches for the name, likely brand comparisons, and descriptions of the dominant shapes. Reviewed official company/brand pages and indexed image-search results, including stock/design marketplaces. This was a preliminary descriptive image search, not a reverse-image upload search, comprehensive trademark-register search, or investigation of the model's training provenance. No exact duplicate was identified in the reviewed results; that is not evidence that no duplicate exists.

## The name needs attention first

- [LOGLY, Inc.](https://corp.logly.co.jp/en) explicitly lists LOGLY Audience Analytics and other data/marketing products. This is exact-name use in a related field.
- [SolarWinds Loggly](https://www.loggly.com/) offers log management and analytics. The close spelling and related subject matter merit attention independently of the symbol.
- [Logly World](https://www.logly.world/) is another exact-name software business, serving museums.

A new symbol does not remove these naming concerns. Keep “logly” as a working label in these boards; recommend a name decision before public brand rollout. These findings do not establish infringement or trademark ownership in any specific jurisdiction.

## Three replacement options

Generated with built-in Imagegen from the bespoke geometric briefs saved in `prompts.json`. Existing-company artwork was not supplied as an image reference. The boards include primary, monochrome, reverse and app-icon treatments. Geometry still needs normalization in final vector artwork.

### 1. A — Offset Register — recommended

![Offset Register](/Users/M1PRO/Documents/code/logly/.design/brand-options/revision-2/a-offset-register.png)

Two unequal components create an off-center stepped channel. Best balance of recognizable silhouette, relevance to ordered records, and continuity with the current green palette. Works without its color. Search results did not reveal an exact match, but angular LC/BL monograms are a crowded category: [LC example](https://www.vectorstock.com/royalty-free-vector/lc-logo-monogram-with-up-to-down-style-modern-vector-30408361), [BL example](https://www.vectorstock.com/royalty-free-vector/bl-logo-monogram-with-up-to-down-style-negative-vector-30241352). Its distinguishing features are the stepped foot and reversed rectangular upper component; preserve those in refinement. Provisional recommendation, not a uniqueness certificate. Test the channel at 16/24/32px before adoption.

### 2. B — Cut Paper — reserve

![Cut Paper](/Users/M1PRO/Documents/code/logly/.design/brand-options/revision-2/b-cut-paper.png)

Strong single silhouette with a clipped corner and two incisions. More visually compact than A, but reads as an elephant/building instead of an event receipt. Searches found the general blue geometric elephant category in [HEFT Cranes](https://heftcranes.com/) and [BrandCrowd's examples](https://www.brandcrowd.com/blog/219-animal-logos-for-a-memorable-brand/). No exact match established. Rank second for simplicity, but the unintended animal reading makes it a weaker product fit.

### 3. C — Paired Imprint — least preferred

![Paired Imprint](/Users/M1PRO/Documents/code/logly/.design/brand-options/revision-2/c-paired-imprint.png)

Two diagonally offset glyphs have a distinctive rhythm, but the actual generated mark reads like “69” or quotation marks. Searches found the broader [geometric 69 category](https://www.vectorstock.com/royalty-free-vectors/number-69-vectors). No exact match established. Rank third because this reading distracts from analytics and the paired counters complicate tiny-size use.

## Recommendation and remaining verification

Choose **A** as the design direction to refine, not as a cleared commercial identity. Resolve the Logly name, normalize A into a consistent vector, and perform image-similarity and relevant national/regional trademark searches on that exact artwork and selected name. [WIPO's Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index) supports image similarity; [USPTO's clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) explains why a public web search alone is insufficient. Jurisdictions and intended goods/services remain unspecified; no filing or legal conclusion is made here.

No production assets or product contracts were changed.
