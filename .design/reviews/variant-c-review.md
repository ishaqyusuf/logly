# Variant C review — Control Room

Score: **82/100**  
Recommendation: **Reserve for a future diagnostics mode**

## First impression

I notice throughput, rejection risk, and ingest boundaries immediately. It is the most operational and the fastest to scan for a technical operator.

## Strengths

- Excellent density with little wasted space.
- Makes rejected batches and trust boundaries visible.
- Clear server/browser/read separation.
- Works naturally in dark mode.

## Weaknesses

- Feels more like observability infrastructure than focused product analytics.
- “Accepted,” “Rejected,” and “Silence” overtake visitor/product questions.
- Dense table and project-health panels compete with the chart.
- Less approachable for a future micro-SaaS customer who is not the operator who built it.

## Litmus checks

Product unmistakable: partial. Strong visual anchor: yes. Scanable headings: yes. One job per section: partial. Cards necessary: yes. Motion useful: neutral. Premium without shadows: yes.

## Best element to carry forward

Use the explicit delivery/rejection/last-event signals inside Collection Health. Do not make the entire default dashboard a control room.
