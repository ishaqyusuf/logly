## First impression

I notice the strongest editorial hierarchy of the three: “Usage is rising, led by service work” answers a question immediately. The wide chart feels calm and distinctive. The compact rail reduces chrome.

## Strengths

- Best at translating metrics into a human conclusion.
- Least template-like visual rhythm.

## Weaknesses

- The result depends on computed narrative logic that does not yet exist.
- Metrics appear after the chart, slowing exact-value lookup.
- Icon-only rail weakens wayfinding for infrequent use and hides project switching.
- Warm rust accent diverges from Logly’s current green trust language.

## Litmus checks

Product unmistakable: yes. Strong visual anchor: yes. Scanable headings: yes. One job per section: yes. Cards necessary: yes. Motion useful: neutral. Premium without shadows: yes.
